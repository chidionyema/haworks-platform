## CRITICAL: Version guard silently drops saved-search notifications on retry
- **File**: `src/Search/Search.Application/Consumers/ProductCacheInvalidatedConsumer.cs:54`
- **Issue**: The out-of-order `SourceVersion` guard can't distinguish "already fully processed" from "index updated, percolate/publish failed." A retry after a post-upsert failure re-fetches the doc, sees `SourceVersion >= incomingVersion`, and returns before ever reaching `PercolateAsync`/`Publish` (lines 92-104).
- **Impact**: Saved-search match events are permanently and silently lost — no error, no DLQ, just a benign-looking INFO log.
- **Fix**: Track percolation/publish completion separately from indexing (e.g. a `LastPercolatedVersion` field, or always percolate+publish even when the version guard short-circuits the upsert).
